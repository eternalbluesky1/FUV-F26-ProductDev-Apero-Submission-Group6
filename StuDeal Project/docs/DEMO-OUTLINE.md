# Demo Outline

Target: 2 to 3 minutes, recorded from the running app, with the data mode visible.

1. Home: state the problem in one sentence and show the category entry points.
2. Start: choose three interests, then continue.
3. Deck: save one offer, skip one, open the conditions of one.
4. Summary: show the unique saved count and the Pocket path.
5. Pocket: remove one saved offer, then show the empty or remaining state.
6. Discover: search for a brand and filter by category.
7. Ask Mon: ask for a cinema or software offer. Show the live AI answer and its catalog-backed cards.
8. Detail: open the official source through the outbound action. Do not call it a purchase.
9. Settings: show local-data clearing and the analytics opt-out.
10. Close: state what is live, what uses fixture data, and what remains blocked.

Do not show a video link until the recording exists. Do not imply a partnership, redemption, or live analytics event without evidence.
